T, without fillets:
J=(b tf^3+(h-tf/2) tw^3)/3
Iw=b^3 tf^3/144+(h-tf/2)^3 tw^3/36
shear centre at the intersection of plate middle lines.
The source explicitly notes that warping of tees/angles is small and often
neglected. A nonzero Iw is not, by itself, a Model bug. These are thin-wall
approximations, not exact Saint-Venant solutions for thick solids.

C310x31: torsion assumes parallel flanges and rounds d'=292.3 to292.
Its supplied centroid17.5 mm does not equal the19.711... mm centroid of
the parallel-flange rectangular idealization. The test therefore checks
the absolute shear-centre coordinate using the supplied centroid, plus
the full-precision torsion/warping equations. It does not claim that the
source and the rectangular idealization have identical centroids.

Exact Wagner integral for the sharp T:
S=integral[(x^2+y^2)y dA], centroidal coordinates;
zj_geometric=ys-S/(2 Ix)=-betaX/2 (CISC equation3).
For a rectangle of width b, depth d, centroid ordinate y:
S_rect=b*d*y*(y^2+d^2/4+b^2/12).
This replaces the former20x4/4x30 midpoint quadrature.

Sign qualification, independent of a SAP report:
ResultBeamForces defines moments on the positive section face about axes1/2
by the right-hand rule. M1=integral(y*sigma dA), tension-positive sigma.
Thus positive M1 tensions the top flange and compresses the free stem tip.
The old Mcr implementation assigned the opposite compression-side parameter
and sign of zj. Both are corrected. Zero M1 chooses the more adverse
compressed-stem direction for the otherwise auxiliary calculation.

Independent eigenproblem in TeeTorsionReferenceTests:
Uniform moment, fork supports, free warping, elastic prismatic beam.
Use a sine mode for lateral deflection and twist. With k=pi/L,
Ny=k^2 E Iz, Kt=GJ+k^2 EIw and
g=integral[(y-yc)/Ix * (x^2+(y-ys_absolute)^2) dA],
determinant=Ny*(Kt+M1*g)-M1^2=0.
The tests integrate g using a3x3 Gaussian rule over the two rectangles and
solve the positive critical magnitude by bisection for each moment sign.
They do not call the production Wagner integral or its radical expression.
18 eigenproblems cover both signs, three lengths and three geometric scales.
One order check verifies the compressed-stem critical moment is lower.
One rational-integral check and two unsupported-geometry checks complete
the mechanical tests. Three published property examples are also tested.

Scope: Mcr for sharp parallel-flange T, principal axis1=geometricX.
Rounded/tapered T and wide T with axis1 along geometricY are refused
explicitly: the existing two-rectangle/symmetry formula does not qualify
them. The source does not validate general biaxial member interaction.
The previously tabulated C3 values for nonuniform moments still require
full source qualification; the exact eigenproblem above has C1=C3=1,C2=0.
