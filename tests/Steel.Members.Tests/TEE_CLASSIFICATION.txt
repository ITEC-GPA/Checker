T CLASSIFICATION INVESTIGATION - 2026-10-20

Target: EN1993p11Test.Sap_SectionTTest, without changing its inputs/assertions.
The initial implementation classifies both the flange and stem as uniformly
compressed outstands for every load case. This is a conservative fallback,
not the Table 5.2 classification for a partially compressed stem.

Independent references, first-generation Eurocodes:
1. EN 1993-1-1:2005 + AC:2006/2009, 5.5.2 and Table 5.2 sheet 2,
   printed pp.40 and 43 (PDF pp.42 and 45).
   https://unaribas.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/eurocode-3.pdf
   alpha is the compressed fraction in the PLASTIC distribution for classes
   1/2: limits 9/alpha,10/alpha for the compressed free tip; limits
   9/(alpha*sqrt(alpha)),10/(alpha*sqrt(alpha)) for the tension free tip,

Scope of this first step:
Sharp parallel-flange SectionT, N plus bending about geometric X, both signs.
Principal-axis mapping must be explicit for wide tees.
Flange classified conservatively as uniformly compressed when any part of it
is compressed; fully tensile flange can be omitted.
Biaxial loading, geometric Y bending and worked corners/tapered subclasses
retain the existing fallback. This does not certify their classification.
